Electronic Components

Resistor

A resistor is a component that opposes the flow of electric current.

LED (Light Emitting Diode)

An LED emits light when current flows through it.

Battery

A battery provides electrical energy to power circuits.

Capacitor

A capacitor stores electrical energy in an electric field.

Diode

A diode allows current to flow in one direction only.

Transistor

A transistor is used to amplify or switch electronic signals.

ECE Basics

Voltage

Voltage is the electric potential difference measured in Volts (V).

Current

Current is the flow of electric charge measured in Amperes (A).

Resistance

Resistance opposes the flow of current, measured in Ohms (Ω).

Power

Power is the rate of energy consumption measured in Watts (W).

AC and DC

DC flows in one direction. AC alternates direction periodically.
